Example Law Firm: website brief

A starting point for your designer, writer and the firm’s appointed reviewer.

Audience and objective
- Owner-managed businesses looking for ongoing legal support.
- Visitors can find the right practice team and understand the enquiry process.

Practice information to prepare
- Commercial: explain the matters you handle, the appropriate team and how an initial enquiry works.
- Employment: explain the matters you handle, the appropriate team and how an initial enquiry works.

The first enquiry
- Primary action: Request a callback
- Ask only for enough information to route the enquiry. Keep detailed matter information within the firm’s approved intake process.
- Explain what happens after an enquiry and avoid implying that submitting a form establishes a client relationship.

Material for internal approval
- Approved team biographies and named practice contacts.
- Arrange a review of any fee information and publication obligations with the firm’s responsible person.
- Have the responsible person approve final claims, practice descriptions, confidentiality wording and any relevant regulatory information.
