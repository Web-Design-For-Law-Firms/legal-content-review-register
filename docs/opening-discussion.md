# Try Law Firm Website Brief Builder and share what would make it more useful

This free browser tool is for law firm partners and practice managers. Bring your practice areas, client needs and enquiry process into a concise website brief.

[Open the tool](https://web-design-for-law-firms.github.io/legal-content-review-register/). No coding knowledge, installation or account is needed. Start with the fictional example, or fill in the labelled fields with your own information. A content and enquiry brief for your website project, ready for internal review.

Feedback about unclear questions, missing practical options or mobile usability is welcome here. Please use fictional information in public examples and keep customer or confidential details out of this discussion.

This supports website planning. It does not provide legal advice or assess regulatory compliance.

Maintained by [Web Design for Law Firms](https://webdesignforlawfirms.co.uk/), operated by JW Digital Services Ltd. The original developer helpers are still available in the repository, but they are not needed to use the browser tool.
